## Description
The Zone wears on your mind. Psy health slowly drains from stress, and you have to rest in safe places to recover. Remake of livvan's Psy Stress for G.A.M.M.A.

## Features
- Stress grows in combat, from hits and wounds, near anomalies, in surges and psi storms, underground, at night, and from hunger, thirst and lack of sleep. Every factor is MCM configurable.
- Stress falls in emission cover and friendly camps. Psy health recovers while calm, in sleep, with psy boosters, psy-protecting artefacts, and fully with anabiotic.
- Campfires: choose "Campfires reduce stress" or "Campfires regenerate psy health", each with its own strength slider. MCM configurable.
- Low psy health brings blur, voices and phantoms; at zero you die or turn into a zombie. Thresholds, blur and voice volume are MCM configurable.
- Phantoms look like real mutants (with xcvb's phantoms mods from G.A.M.M.A.) or classic phantoms. MCM configurable.
- Psy damage preset (Uneasy ... Nightmare) and psy regeneration preset (Steady ... Broken), MCM configurable. Controllers, psy fields and the DAO / Arrival psy aura follow the damage preset.
- Fix: getting shot or bitten causes psy stress again (GAMMA's damage balancer hid those hits).
- D.O.G.M.A.: its campfire psy healing is turned off so it doesn't mix with this mod; its body and limb healing still work.

## Install / remove
- Place it **below** "G.A.M.M.A. Psy rework" in MO2. Disable older Psy Stress installs and the separate GAMMA hit patch.
- **Safe to install and remove mid-playthrough**: same save data as G.A.M.M.A. Psy rework, no new items.
